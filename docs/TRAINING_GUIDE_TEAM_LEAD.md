# Team Lead Training Guide: ArchiveOps

Welcome to ArchiveOps! This guide covers the primary tasks for Team Leads using the system.

## 1. How to log in with Microsoft SSO
1. Navigate to the ArchiveOps platform (e.g., `https://archiveops.dataguardng.com`).
2. Click on the **Sign in with Microsoft** button.
3. Enter your `@dataguardng.com` credentials.
4. Complete any required Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA).

## 2. How to submit your own daily report
1. From the dashboard, navigate to **My Reports**.
2. Click **Submit New Report**.
3. Fill out the relevant fields (project, units processed, time, etc.).
4. Click **Submit**.

## 3. How to register a new ad-hoc worker
1. Navigate to the **Worker Management** section.
2. Click **Add New Worker**.
3. Enter the worker's Full Name and Phone Number (starting with `+234` or `0`).
4. Select the project they are assigned to.
5. Click **Save**.

## 4. How to send WhatsApp onboarding to ad-hoc workers
1. Navigate to the **Worker Management** section.
2. Select the workers you want to onboard.
3. Click **Send Onboarding Message**.
4. This will trigger a WhatsApp message with the form link to their registered phone number.

## 5. How to submit a report on behalf of an ad-hoc worker
1. If a worker cannot submit via their phone, go to **Team Submissions**.
2. Click **Manual Entry**.
3. Select the worker's name from the dropdown.
4. Fill in the daily production data as provided by the worker.
5. Click **Submit**.

## 6. How to review and approve pending ad-hoc submissions
1. Navigate to **Approvals** on your dashboard.
2. You will see a list of pending submissions from your team.
3. Review the reported numbers.
4. Click **Approve** to confirm or **Flag** to mark for discrepancy review.

## 7. How to view the WhatsApp Sandbox log
1. If you have admin privileges, navigate to **System Logs**.
2. Select **WhatsApp Communications**.
3. Review the history of outgoing messages and responses.

## 8. How to export PDF/Excel reports
1. Go to the **Reports** section.
2. Select the date range and project.
3. Click the **Export** button in the top right corner.
4. Choose either **PDF** or **Excel** format.
