# Ad-hoc Worker Guide / Guide for Ad-hoc Workers

## English Section
Welcome! Follow these simple steps to submit your daily work report.

1. You will receive a WhatsApp message with a Google Form link.
2. Open the link in your phone's browser.
3. Fill in your name and phone number.
4. Enter the number of boxes/bags/crates you processed today.
5. Enter the number of files you processed.
6. If you scanned documents, enter the number of pages.
7. If you indexed records, enter the number indexed.
8. If there were any problems, describe them in the notes.
9. Tap **Submit**.
10. You're done! Your Team Lead will review your report.

### FAQ
**Q: What if I didn't work today?**
A: You do not need to submit a form for days you do not work.

**Q: Can I submit the form twice in one day?**
A: Please only submit once at the end of your shift with your total numbers for the day.
